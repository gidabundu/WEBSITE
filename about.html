A PLACE WITH A POINT OF VIEW

Good food starts
with good questions.

Noir Kitchen began in 2009 with a simple idea: create a Paris dining room that feels both deeply grounded and genuinely curious. One that honours the ingredient, welcomes the unexpected, and makes room for connection.

Our cooking follows the seasons, our growers, and the instinct to keep learning. Nothing stands still for long—and that's exactly how we like it.

MEET CHEF ÉRIC

A lifetime at
the pass.

Chef Éric's path has taken him from the kitchens of Michel Bras to Mugaritz and onward to Japan, where he found inspiration in a different relationship with seasonality, restraint, and detail.

Back in Paris, he brings those perspectives together with a distinctly French sensibility—cooking that is generous, precise, and always in conversation with the moment.
